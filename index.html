ABOUT PLANT ATLAS

A map-first plant reference.

Plant Atlas is organized around a simple question: where is a plant from, where has it spread, and what evidence supports those labels?

TaxonomyKew Plants of the World Online / WCVP
OccurrencesGBIF and other biodiversity repositories
Invasive statusCABI plus regional authorities

Important: the included catalog uses demonstration country ranges. Treat it as interface data until you replace ranges with source-backed geographic datasets.
SOURCES & METHODOLOGY

What should count as a range?

Use authoritative distribution sources for native and introduced ranges, then keep invasive status separately sourced. Occurrence records are evidence of observations, not automatic proof that a species is native or invasive.

Native rangeUse taxonomic/distribution references such as Kew POWO/WCVP and regional floras.
Introduced rangeUse documented naturalization or introduction records with regional sources where available.
Invasive rangeOnly mark locations backed by an invasive-species authority or clearly cited scientific source.
